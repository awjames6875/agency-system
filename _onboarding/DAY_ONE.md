# Day One — New Agent Onboarding Guide

Welcome to Diana's team. This guide gets you operational on the AI operating system in one day. No technical background required.

---

## The Big Picture (5 minutes)

This system has 5 AI specialists. Each one owns a specific part of your workflow. They pass work to each other automatically through handoff contracts.

Your job is not to manage the AI. Your job is to:
1. **Receive routed work** from the right specialist
2. **Review AI-drafted outputs** before they go to clients
3. **Escalate anything you're unsure about** to Diana

You are always in the loop. Nothing goes to a client without your eyes on it.

---

## The 5 Specialists (2 minutes each)

**00_orchestrator** — The front door. Every inbound request lands here. You don't interact with this directly — it routes work to you.

**01_lead_qualifier** — Handles first contact with new prospects. When a new lead comes in, this specialist captures their intent, budget, and timeline. You receive a clean qualified lead ready to act on.

**02_property_research** — Pulls comps, neighborhood data, and talking points. You receive a research brief before any client showing or call.

**03_client_communication** — Drafts messages in YOUR voice. You receive a draft, review it, and send it. You always send — never automated.

**04_transaction_coordinator** — Tracks deadlines and documents once a deal is under contract. You receive alerts before deadlines — not after.

---

## Your Daily Workflow

### A new lead comes in
1. You receive a `qualified_lead` notification in GHL
2. Review the qualification notes
3. Use the research brief from `02_property_research` to prep for your first call
4. Review and send the draft from `03_client_communication`
5. Log your notes in GHL

### A deal is under contract
1. `04_transaction_coordinator` loads all deadlines automatically
2. You receive GHL task alerts 48-72 hours before each deadline
3. When the TC needs a client update, it sends you a draft via `03_client_communication` — review and send
4. If anything looks wrong, escalate to Diana

### Something you're unsure about
Escalate. The system has an escalation path built in. Use it. Diana would rather get an escalation than have you guess.

---

## Common Scenarios

| What's happening | Where to look |
|---|---|
| New lead just came in | GHL pipeline → New Lead stage → qualified_lead notes |
| Need to call a client | Research brief in GHL or `02_property_research` output |
| Need to text/email a client | Draft is waiting in `03_client_communication` — review and send |
| Inspection issue on active deal | TC has already flagged it — check GHL tasks |
| Competing offer | TC trigger → draft in client_comm with recommended position |
| Don't know what to do | Ask Diana. Always. |

---

## What You Should NEVER Do

1. Send a client message without reviewing the draft
2. Reassign a lead to a different agent without Diana's approval
3. Make a legal recommendation to a client
4. Ignore a GHL deadline alert

---

## You're Ready When...

- You can find a new lead in GHL and know which specialist produced it
- You've reviewed a research brief and used it in a client call
- You've reviewed and sent a client_communication draft
- You know where to find the escalation path

If you've done those four things, you're operational. Ask Diana for anything else.
